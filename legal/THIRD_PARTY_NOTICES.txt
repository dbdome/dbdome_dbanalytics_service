--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                  (b) Phi-3.1-mini-128k-instruct-Q4_K_M.gguf (GGUF quantization)
Publisher:      Microsoft Corporation
Homepage:       https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-3-mini-128k-instruct
License:        MIT License
How used:       Executed locally on the customer's own hardware by the DBDOME

                is not fine-tuned or retrained by DBDOME; it is prompted at
                inference time. In the default configuration no query text,
                schema, credential or customer data is transmitted to Microsoft,
                to DBEXPERT, or to any external service.
Modifications:  The model weights are not modified. Form (b) is a quantization
                of the published weights to the GGUF format.

    MIT License

    Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation.

    [Full MIT License text: see PART 6, section 6.1]
